## About

I'm Pete Roome, CTO at [Carpata](https://carpata.com) where we're building AI-native tools for automotive parts procurement.

Before Carpata, I worked across product and engineering at companies like Cazoo, Lyst, and several startups I co-founded. I've spent most of my career at the intersection of product and technology -- figuring out what to build and then building it.

This blog is where I write about what I'm learning. Recently that's been focused on AI-native product development -- how to build products where AI is a core capability rather than a bolt-on feature.

You can find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/peteroome/) or subscribe via [RSS](/feed.xml).
